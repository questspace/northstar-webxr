VIBESTAR REVISION 0: JOINT FIT COUPONS

These two parts test a folding joint. They do not attach to the optical frame
and do not establish wearable fit or load capacity. STL coordinates are mm.
The package manifest records the exact STL hashes and assumptions.

Starting process: PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, four perimeters, 35%
infill. Set temperatures and cooling from your filament manufacturer's
profile. Do not scale the parts in the slicer to compensate for a tight bore.

Arm: place its broad, unchannelled face on the bed, cable channel upward.
Fork: use the flat backing face as the bed face; inspect layer preview for
the curved tab overhangs and add removable support if your printer needs it.
Use the slicer's lay-on-face tool, not the original CAD orientation.

After cooling, remove supports and burrs. Measure the bore, arm thickness
and tab gap. Nominal values: 3.2 mm bore, 6.0 mm arm, 6.6 mm gap.
Record measurements before changing the recipe. The 0.3 mm gap per side
is a starting clearance, not a guarantee of fit on your printer.

Align the two pivot bores with the arm between the tabs. Trial-assemble
with an M3 through screw, washers and a prevailing-torque nut. Select screw
length from the actual 12.6 mm outer fork stack plus washers and nut
engagement. Do not clamp the printed tabs inward until they bind the arm.
Check smooth 0-90 degree rotation and inspect for cracking or delamination.
Keep all ribbon and USB cables outside the joint during this test.

Pass criteria for this coupon: inserts without forcing, turns through the
full range, has no visible cracks, and the screw can be retained without
binding. Record play and required torque. This is not a fatigue or strength
qualification, and there is no mechanical open-position stop yet.

Tune the CAD dimensions, regenerate, export and rerun package_fitprints.py.
Keep reference optical mounting geometry unchanged during these tests.
