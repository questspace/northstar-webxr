VIBESTAR SUPPORT ASSEMBLY — FIT PROTOTYPE, REVISION 1

This set adds six removable parts to the candidate NSNF_v01 carrier. It is
not the finished glasses enclosure. Reuse the existing optical carrier;
do not remove or relocate optics to install this fit trial.

Files use millimetres and assembly coordinates. In the slicer, separate
parts and place each on the bed; do not scale. The rear temples bend inward
to 150 mm centre-to-centre ear spacing. This is an assumed starting fit.
The candidate front remains about 234 mm wide.

Print assumptions: PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, four perimeters,
35% infill. Print a rail saddle/cap and one hinge pair before a full set.
Lay the temple's broad surface on the bed; the ear drop requires an
orientation/support choice. Keep support scars out of bearing surfaces.
The fork has horizontal shelves: inspect support removal and layer bonding.
No slicer profile or structural test has validated these assumptions.

Hardware starting set:
- Eight M3 x 40 mm through bolts, sixteen M3 washers, eight M3 nuts for
  the rail clamps. Confirm grip length and thread projection on the print.
- Two M3 x 20 mm hinge bolts, four washers, two M3 locknuts. Confirm
  washer stack and usable thread length before tightening.
- Approximately 0.5 mm compliant shim under each retained rail. The slot
  is 8.2 mm wide; the candidate rail is 7.55 mm wide and 26.9 mm tall.
- Optional soft rear retention strap and soft temple contact sleeves.
  Sleeve dimensions depend on the actual wearer and material.

Slide each saddle around its corresponding existing rail, place the shim
under the rail and fit the cap. Tighten evenly only enough to prevent slip.
A cap bottoming on the saddle without gripping the rail means the shim
stack needs adjustment. Do not bend or crush the retained rail.

Fit the 6 mm temple tab between the fork leaves. Nominal axial clearance
is 0.3 mm per side. The 3.2 mm pivot bore is a clearance hole, not a thread.
Use a locknut to retain adjustment; do not clamp the printed fork closed.
Support the frame independently during the first fit trial. Hinges do not
yet have mechanical opening stops; temple friction alone is not retention.

The 6 mm wide, 2 mm deep open channel is for routing trials. It does not
establish that the retained USB/video bundle fits. Keep a service loop
outside the pivot and secure the cable without loading its connectors.
A permanent harness needs strain relief and folding/pull testing.

Validation performed: six single connected closed oriented STL meshes;
ClassCAD OFB, STEP and STL exports; assembly displayed with nine reference
instances. No collision sweep, minimum-wall, fatigue, drop, optical,
electrical, thermal or on-head validation has been completed.

Still required for complete glasses: nose contact/support, opening stops,
front electronics housing, registered sensor mount and optical surfaces,
validated harness and portable power/compute enclosure. Keep the original
calibrated optical structure until those interfaces are established.
